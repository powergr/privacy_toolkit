Key Features

File Encryption

Secure photos, documents, and videos with AES-256-GCM. Our streaming engine handles files of unlimited size (100GB+) without slowing down your PC or phone.

Password Vault

Store your passwords, recovery codes, and sensitive notes in an encrypted vault. Zero-Knowledge and completely offline.

Secure Shredder

Permanently destroy sensitive files before selling a laptop or USB drive. Uses 3-pass overwriting to prevent data recovery tools from working.
